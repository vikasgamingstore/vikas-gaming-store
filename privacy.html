Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026
Info We Collect How We Use It Payment Data Sharing Security Your Rights Contact

1. Information We Collect

When you shop with Vikas Gaming Store, we collect information you provide directly, such as your name, mobile number, email address, delivery address, and order details. We also collect basic usage data like pages visited and items viewed, to help us improve the store.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To send order updates, tracking information, and support messages (including via WhatsApp)
  • To respond to your queries and provide customer support
  • To improve our product catalog, pricing, and site experience

3. Payment Information

Booking payments are made directly by you via UPI to our published UPI ID / QR code. We do not store your UPI PIN, bank account details, or card numbers. You may be asked to share your UPI transaction ID (UTR) to confirm your payment — this is used solely to verify and process your order.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Your details may be shared with delivery partners solely to fulfil your order, or disclosed if required by law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of online transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting our support team. You can also update your saved addresses and profile details directly from your account dashboard.

7. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, reach out at the email on our Contact page or via WhatsApp.
